A Letter For You

To My Wifey, Xenia

My dearest Xenia,

Happy Birthday and Happy 3rd Monthsary, my Wifey. Ang wish ko lang for you ay good health palagi. Please take care of yourself always bebe and mag ingat ikaw palagi kahit saan ka man ikaw pumunta. Sana rin gumaan kahit papano yung situation mo jan sa family mo. I know na hindi naman lahat ng bagay ay madali kaya sana malagpasan mo rin lahat ng challenges na dumadaan sa buhay mo and wag sana ikaw susuko at lalong wag mo hayaang mapahinto ka sa pag-aaral mo bebe. Gusto kong makita ikaw makatapos at maabot yung mga goals mo and maging proud ikaw sa sarili mo someday.

More than anything, gusto ko lang na maging genuinely happy ikaw palagi. Sana kahit may mga bad days, may dahilan parin ikaw ngumiti. Sana hindi mo makalimutan na may taong nandito palagi para sayo na willing makinig sayo, umintindi sayo, at sumuporta sayo. I'll always be here to remind you na hindi mo kailangan harapin lahat na mag-isa. And of course I wish na matupad mo lahat ng dreams mo sa buhay, kahit ano pa man mga yan. Sana makuha mo lahat ng bagay na deserve mo and sana dummating yung time na masasabi mong "worth it lahat ng pinagdaanan ko."

And of course bebe I just want to say na happy ako na dumating ka sa life ko. Thank you talaga dahil inangat mo ako mula sa madilim kong buhay. Thank you for loving me and caring for me so mcuh. Hindi ko talaga inaakala na may taong kayang magbigay sa akin ng ganitong klaseng love, yung love na sobrang tagal ko hinahanap at hinihintay hanggang sa dumating sa point na sinukuan ko na at inisip ko na hindi ko na talaga mararanasan yung ganung love.

Akala ko hindi na darating yung taong mamahalin ako nang ganito, but I was wrong. Pinatunayan mo sa akin na may taong kaya ako mahalin nang buong-buo and I never imagine na yung taong magbibigay sakin nun ay ikaw. Hanggang ngayon bebe hindi parin ako makapaniwala sa lahat ng nangyayari satin bebe.

Im so lucky to have you. Para kang isang anghel na dumating sa buhay ko, isang napakaganda at espesyal na anghel na hindi ko kailanman gustong mawala. Thank you so much my wifey for coming into my life and making it so much more colorful.

At hayaan mo bebe makakaasa ka sa akin na gagawin ko ang lahat para maging healthy, peaceful, and happy ang relationship natin. Hinding-hindi kita lolokohin and never na never kong gagawin yung mga bagay na alam kong makakasira sa relationship natin. I'll always choose to protect what we have and take care of what we built together.

I hope this relationship continues to grow and that we become better people together. Sana maging inspiration and motivation natin ang isa't-isa habang inaabot natin mga goals and dreams natin and sana supportahan din natin ang isa't-isa. Hindi naman need maging perfect tayo, basta maging honest, loyal, and faithful lang tayo sa isa't-isa. No lies, no unnecessary drama, and no things that could slowly make our relationship toxic or eventually break us apart. Let’s just behave, understand each other, communicate properly, and focus on ourselves and our future. I want us to grow together, achieve our dreams together, and hopefully, someday in the future, maging mag-asawa talaga tayo.

I love you so much, my beautiful future wife, my angel, my person, my special one, my bebe, my Xenia. Thank you for being you. Thank you for choosing me. And thank you for loving me the way you do. Happy birthday bebe and happy 3rd monthsary to us! I’m so excited for all the months and years that we’ll still share together. I love you always and forever. Mwahhh!

Forever yours,

Jevsson
